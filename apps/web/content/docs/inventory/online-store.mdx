# Selling online

Your website reads products, prices and stock from BookOne and sends orders back. This page is for the people who keep the catalog in BookOne. Developers connecting a website should read [Custom Website API](/docs/developers/custom-website).

## 1. Product details for websites

Open a product (**Inventory → Products → edit**) and use the **Online store** tab:

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Weight (grams)** | Websites charge delivery by parcel weight. Fill it in for every product you sell online. |
| Length / width / height, Fragile | For couriers that price by size, and careful packing. |
| **Short name** | Shown on product cards and in the cart. |
| **Product brand** | The maker (e.g. Winsor & Newton). Add brands under **Inventory → Categories → Product brands**. |
| Tags, more barcodes | Search and scanning on the website. |
| Minimum / maximum / step quantity | For example sell in steps of 6. |
| Website may take orders when out of stock | Backorders for this product. |
| **Specifications** | One per line as `code=value` (e.g. `volume_ml=8`). Websites use them for filters. |

## 2. Products with colours or sizes (variants)

1. Create the **listing**: a product such as "Cotman 8ml Tube". On **Online store**, tick **This is a listing with variants** and enter the options, e.g. `Colour: Chinese White, Lemon Yellow`. The listing itself is not sold or stocked.
2. Create each **variant** as a normal product with its own SKU, price and stock (e.g. "Cotman 8ml Chinese White"). On **Online store**, choose the listing under **Variant of** and enter this variant's option, e.g. `Colour=Chinese White`. Optionally set a swatch colour and **Show this variant first**.

Each variant keeps its own stock, so a POS sale of Lemon Yellow reduces only Lemon Yellow on the website.

## 3. Photos

On the product page, under **Photos for online stores**, upload clear photos (at least 1600 px on the long side). The first photo is the main one; use **Make main**, ↑ and ↓ to reorder, and add a short description (alt text) for each. The first photo also becomes the POS photo if the product has none.

## 4. Choose what each website sells

**Company → Commerce Integrations → Products**:

- **Products on this store**: filter by category or brand, tick products, **Publish selected** / **Unpublish selected**. Publishing a listing publishes all its variants.
- **Missing weight** lists published products without a weight.
- Products already linked to existing website listings count as published.

**Settings → Stock shown to the website**:

- **Safety buffer**: keep units back from the website (e.g. 1, so the shop keeps the last one).
- **Backorders**: let the website take orders when stock is 0.

Changes reach the website within seconds through its change feed.

## 5. Prices for websites

**Company → Commerce Integrations → Prices**:

- **Prices on this store**: choose the price list this website sells at. With no list, the website uses each product's BookOne sell price.
- **Price lists**: create a list per channel (e.g. *Online Retail*) or per customer group (e.g. *wholesale*). A website can show a group's prices to signed-in customers of that group.
- **Edit prices** on a list: set a list price, a **Was price** (shown struck through), a **Sale price** with from/to dates, and **Quantity prices** such as `6=1050; 12=990` (unit price from 6 and from 12). Leave a price empty to use the sell price.
- **Set every price from the sell price ±%** fills the whole list at once, e.g. `-10` for 10% off. **Clear all prices** empties the list.

Sales start at the beginning of the first day and end at the end of the last day, in the company timezone. Websites see price changes within seconds. If an order arrives at a different price (for example after a coupon), BookOne still accepts it and records the price the customer paid.

## 6. Web orders

Orders from the website appear on **Commerce Integrations → Orders**. Cash-on-delivery and bank-transfer orders hold their stock as soon as they arrive, so the same item cannot be sold again at the POS. From an order you can **Confirm**, **Mark paid** (e.g. when the courier remits cash), **Dispatch** with courier and tracking number, **Mark delivered**, **Cancel** (releases or restocks), and **Refund** (with or without restock). The customer's website sees each change.
