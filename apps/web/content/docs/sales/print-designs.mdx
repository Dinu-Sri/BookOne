# Print designs

**Company → Print designs** decides how each document looks when you print it. Every print item starts on the **BookOne standard** print. You can keep it, edit a design here, or ask your AI assistant to make one, even from a photo of an invoice you like.

Nothing changes on paper until **you approve** a design.

## Print items

Quotation, proforma invoice, invoice, VAT tax invoice, payment receipt, credit note, sales order / dispatch note, packing slip, POS bill, purchase order and payment voucher. Each has its own designs.

## What every design must keep

Some parts are **required** and cannot be removed, so every print stays correct:

- every item: your company name, the document title, its number and date;
- invoices and quotations: the customer, the items and the total;
- **VAT tax invoice**: the title "Tax invoice", your address and TIN, the purchaser's name, address and TIN, the place of supply, the payment mode, each item with quantity and value, the value before VAT, the VAT, the total and the amount in words.

Everything else (logo, bank details, terms, signature lines, QR code, your own text) is optional.

## Make a design yourself

1. Open **Company → Print designs** and pick the print item on the left.
2. Press **New design**. It starts from the BookOne starting design.
3. Change the paper, font, colours and parts. The preview on the right shows your company details with sample items.
4. Press **Save as new version**, check the preview, then press **Approve and use**.

## Let your AI assistant design it

1. Connect Claude or Codex in **Company → AgentBridge**, then press **Change abilities** on the assistant and turn on **Print designs**.
2. In chat, send a photo or screenshot of the invoice you like and ask: "Design my invoice like this."
3. The assistant saves a draft and asks you to review it. A yellow note on Print designs shows designs waiting for you.
4. Open it, check the preview and press **Approve and use**.

The assistant can save new designs and new versions. It can never delete a design or put one in use.

## Logo and images

Upload your **logo**, a **signature** and a **company stamp** under **Logo and images**. If a design shows a logo you have not uploaded, BookOne warns you and the space stays empty.

## Versions

Every save is a new version; earlier versions are kept. Documents keep the design they were first printed with, so a reprint of last month's invoice looks the same. To go back to the standard print, press **Use BookOne standard**. Only a person can delete a version, and not the one in use.
